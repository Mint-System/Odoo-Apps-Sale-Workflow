Purchase to order:

- Install purchase_stock_sale_owner
- Enable consignment, storage locations and multistep routes
- Unarchive "Replenish on Order (MTO)"
- Create serial tracked product "Bicycle" with route mto and buy
- Create an SO for the product and confirm it
- Confirm the PO and set a serial number on the receipt
- Confirm the receipt and open the delivery
- Ensure serial number is set and confirm the delivery

Sale only:

- In settings activate stock locations and consignment
- Create a sale order for "Deco Addict"
- Add a new product "Switch" with 10 quantities
- Set quantity 1 on the so line and confirm
- Open the delivery and confirm it
- Ensure the product is in the customer location
- Then make return for the delivery
- Ensure the product quantity is WH/Stock not assigned to the customer
